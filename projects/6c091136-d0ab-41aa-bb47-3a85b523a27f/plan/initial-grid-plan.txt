### Beat 3: The Value of Handmade Pottery (0:32 – 0:49)

* **Goal:** Explain the tactile and emotional value of handmade pottery in contrast with mass consumption.

* **Interview / Reflection:** Transition into the deeper philosophical value of ceramics. Viviana reflects on physical mediums as a tool for human connection, and explains the tactile concept of the artist's hand: "There's something called the artist's hand where when you hand-build something or wheel throw it, you can kind of feel where the artist's hands have been". Follow with her challenge to mass-consumer culture and questions around handmade pricing: "People are like, well, why is it 50 bucks? I can buy ten mugs with that. And I'm like, but you need ten more mugs because like, do you have ten people in your family?"
